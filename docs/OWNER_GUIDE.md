# Updating the website: a short guide

This guide is for Bharat or a family member who keeps the website up to date. You do not need to touch any code.

## Opening the editor

Go to **your-website-address/keystatic** and sign in with GitHub. The left-hand menu has the everyday tasks
under **Routine updates**:

- **Add photos**
- **Add a video**
- **Add an award**
- **Add a testimonial**
- **Edit contact details**

## Adding a photo

1. Choose **Add photos**, then **Add**.
2. Type a short title. This is only for you.
3. Press **Choose file** and pick the photo.
4. Under **People in the photo**, add each name you are sure of.
5. Write the **caption** that visitors will see, and a short **description** for people who cannot see the photo.
6. Fill in the event, the date (only as precise as you know, e.g. `2019`), and Bharat's role if confirmed.
7. Set **Permission to use** to *Approved* only when you have permission.
8. Set **Visibility** to *Published*, then press **Save**.

The website updates a minute or two after you save.

## Choosing what appears on the home page

Open **Homepage selections** under *Page set-up*. Each list (photos, videos, awards, testimonials) shows what
appears on the home page, in order. Use the item menu to move an item up or down, add one, or remove one.
Removing an item from the home page does not delete it. It stays in the gallery.

## Other text you can edit

Under *Page set-up* you can also change **Services**, **Who Bharat works with** and **Common questions**
(shown on the contact page). Use the item menu to add, remove or reorder entries.

## Hiding something

Open the item and set **Visibility** to *Draft* or *Archived*. It disappears from the website but is not deleted.

## Undoing a change

Every save is recorded. Ask Ryan to restore an earlier version if something goes wrong.

## Why something does not show up

An item appears on the website only when all of these are true:

- **Visibility** is *Published*.
- For photos and videos, **Permission to use** is *Approved*.
- For awards, at least one evidence source is listed and **Bharat has approved this wording** is ticked.
- For testimonials, **The person has approved this quote** is ticked.
- It is not marked as a placeholder.

## Enquiries

Messages sent through the website's contact form arrive by email. Press **Reply** to answer the visitor
directly. If notification emails stop arriving, an alert is sent to the backup address, and the messages
themselves are kept safely on the server.
